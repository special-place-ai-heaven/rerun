A self-hosted coworker is the same coworker as on Rerun Cloud. The pages of the **Documentation** tab apply as they are; this page points you to them, and says what depends on your install.

<Note>
  Where those pages say "machine", read "server": the one your workspace runs on. Where they mention credit, plans or a trial, that does not apply to your install.
</Note>

## The basics

A coworker sits in a Box on the board. It has a name, a handle other coworkers call it by, a soul (its system prompt), a model and its own memory, skills, files and database. You talk to it in a chat, and it can call the other coworkers of the workspace.

**Memory.** A coworker writes down what it learns and recalls it later. Correct it once and it stays corrected. Recall matches by meaning when `RERUN_EMBED_API_KEY` is set, and by keywords otherwise. See [Memory](/concepts#memory).

**The Brain.** The pages about your company that every coworker of the workspace reads: what you sell, to whom, how you work. The Chief of Staff seeds it during onboarding from your answers and your website. Reading the website needs `FIRECRAWL_API_KEY`. Either way, you can write and edit pages yourself.

Scheduled tasks run on your server, so they only fire while it is up. Webhook URLs point at your `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_URL`: a service on the internet can only call them when your app is reachable from the internet.

Notifications show in the app. This build sends no email notification.

## Its computer

Each coworker has a Chromium browser and a screen of its own, on your server. Open **Desktop** from the coworker to watch it work, and take over when it needs your hands, for a captcha or a sign-in.

The browser opens the screen directly on your server's address, so the screen needs that address reachable from your browser. See [The desktop screen](/self-host/servers#the-desktop-screen).

## Templates

A template packages a working coworker setup for someone else to install. The template library of Rerun Cloud does not ship with this build, so your install starts with none. See [Create a template](/templates/create) to package your own.

## The API

Every coworker is reachable from Claude Code, Cursor or your own scripts through the Rerun MCP server, at your own address instead of `app.rerun.build`. See the [API](/api/introduction).
